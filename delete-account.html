Account controls

Delete your account

How to permanently delete your account and the personal data that goes with it.

From the app

You can start permanent account deletion from My details in the iPhone or Android app. This verifies the request against your signed-in account.

If you cannot access the app

Use the contact form below. Choose Privacy, enter the email used for your account, and write “Delete my account”. Support will verify that you own the address before deletion.

Open the deletion request form →

What happens next

Personal data associated with the account is deleted after verification. If you organise an active trip, support will first help close it or transfer the organiser role so the rest of the crew do not lose shared trip information. Records that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting obligations are kept only for the required period.
