The situation

“WE SHOULD GO AWAY.”

It starts exactly the same way every time. Someone drops the idea in the group chat. Everyone is immediately in. Three destinations appear. Four dates get suggested. Somebody finds a villa. Somebody else sends a TikTok.

Then the useful information disappears under 146 messages, nobody remembers who has paid, and the person who suggested the whole thing quietly becomes the organiser.

This is where The Girls Trip Guide takes over.
