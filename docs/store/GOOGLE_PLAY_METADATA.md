## Full description

The Girls Trip Guide is a private group trip planner for keeping the plan, bookings, shared costs, friends and trip evidence together.

Create a trip or join one by invitation. Add travel, accommodation, transfers and booking documents. Track shared costs and payment status without losing the details across several group chats.

Free includes trip planning, group management and standard operational reminders.

Existing Full Trip customers can sign in with the same email to use the trip's shared photo and video features, Evidence, Hidden Gallery and complete GALS communications system. Existing access is recognised securely from the account.

The product remains invitation-only. Trip information and uploaded content are available only to authorised trip members, subject to their role and the trip settings.

The Girls Trip Guide is built by Storystone Ltd.
