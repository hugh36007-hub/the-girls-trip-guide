## Description

The Girls Trip Guide keeps a group trip together without making the organiser live in five different chats.

Set up the trip, invite the girls and keep the important details in one private place. Track dates, destinations, travel, stays, transfers, documents, shared costs, payment status and the people coming with you.

Free includes the complete planning system and standard operational reminders.

Existing Full Trip customers can sign in with the same email to access their trip's shared photo and video features, Evidence, Hidden Gallery and GALS communications. Full Trip access is recognised securely from the account; no separate app account is required.

The service is private by default and invitation-only. Trip information and uploaded media are visible only to authorised members of that trip, subject to their role and the trip's settings.

Key features:

- Create or join a private trip.
- Keep travel, stays, transfers and booking documents together.
- Track shared costs, requests and payment status.
- Manage the group and important trip details.
- Receive useful reminders before, during and after the trip.
- Access shared photos and videos where Full Trip is already active.
- Switch safely between multiple trips from one account.

The Girls Trip Guide is built by Storystone Ltd.

## Review notes

The app uses email one-time passwords. A reviewer email must be added before submission; no production password is stored in the repository.

Suggested review path:

1. Open the app and choose Sign In.
2. Enter the supplied reviewer email.
3. Enter the six-digit OTP delivered to that mailbox.
4. Open the prepared Free trip and inspect Plan, Money and Group.
5. Switch to the prepared Full Trip and inspect shared media and communications.
6. Account deletion is under My details.

Commerce note: the submitted build contains no Stripe checkout, external purchase button or external purchase call to action. Existing web entitlements are recognised after sign-in. Any native purchase implementation will be submitted only after the applicable App Store commercial route is selected.

## Required owner-supplied review access

- Reviewer email inbox access or a controlled OTP forwarding arrangement.
- One prepared Free trip.
- One prepared Full Trip with non-sensitive demonstration media.
