# Store Screenshot Specification

Use real product UI and demonstration data only. Do not fabricate functionality or expose a real customer's trip.

## Apple

The iOS target is intentionally iPhone-only for the first release.

- Provide 6 portrait screenshots.
- Capture at an accepted 6.9-inch size, preferably 1320 x 2868 px or 1290 x 2796 px.
- PNG or JPEG, no alpha channel.
- No device frame is required.

Sequence:

1. Landing - the approved Girls hero and private trip proposition.
2. Dashboard - dates, destination and current trip status.
3. The Plan - travel, stay and transfer details.
4. Money - shared costs and payment tracking.
5. Group - invited members and role-aware controls.
6. The Evidence - non-sensitive demonstration photos in an existing Full Trip.

## Google Play

- Provide at least 4 portrait phone screenshots at 1080 x 1920 px (9:16).
- JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha channel.
- Keep screenshots between 320 px and 3840 px per dimension; the long side must not exceed twice the short side.
- Use actual app UI. Keep added marketing text minimal and do not mention price or discounts.
- Provide a 512 x 512 PNG store icon under 1 MB.

Use the same six-screen story as Apple unless the final Play listing test indicates a shorter set performs better.
