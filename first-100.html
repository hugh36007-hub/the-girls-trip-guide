Questions

Before you overthink it.

Why is Full Trip discounted?

This is controlled early access. The First 100 offer exchanges a much lower launch price for genuine use and useful feedback.

Do I lose any Full Trip features because I paid £5?

No. A qualifying First 100 purchase is the normal Full Trip product at the launch promotion price.

How do I use the discount?

Create the trip Free first. When the organiser chooses Full Trip from inside the trip, enter FIRST100 at Stripe checkout.

Can I just stay on Free?

Yes. The Free trip remains usable. Full Trip is optional.
