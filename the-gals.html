So... how does this actually work?

Four steps to get the trip under control. Then one optional upgrade if you want the GALS involved.

01

Create the trip

How

Add the destination, dates, flights, stays and who’s coming.

Why

It gives everyone one clear plan from the start.

Benefit

Less confusion, fewer repeated questions.

02

Invite the girls

How

Send one private invite so everyone joins the same trip.

Why

The details stop living across five different chats.

Benefit

No chasing screenshots, links or updates.

03

Keep everything together

How

Track money, bookings, reminders, receipts and updates in one place.

Why

Everyone can see what’s booked, what’s paid and what’s next.

Benefit

You stay organised without becoming the trip secretary.

04

Enjoy the trip

How

Use the trip as your shared home for plans, payments and updates while you’re away.

Why

Nobody has to keep asking where the booking is or what happens next.

Benefit

Less admin mid-trip. More actual trip.
