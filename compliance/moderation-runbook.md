# The Girls Trip Guide — moderation and complaints runbook

Version: 4 September 2026  
Online Safety Lead: Hugh Azmi  
Deputy Online Safety Lead: Ediz

1. Record the report time, reporter contact, trip reference, content reference and allegation. Never copy suspected CSAM.
2. Triage:
   - **Emergency:** credible immediate danger — advise 999 and preserve evidence.
   - **Priority:** CSEA, grooming, intimate-image abuse, cyberflashing, terrorism, credible threats, serious self-harm — restrict exposure immediately where possible and escalate to the safety lead.
   - **Standard:** other Terms, privacy or copyright complaints.
3. Use authorised owner controls to inspect only the reported material and surrounding context needed for a fair decision.
4. Preserve minimum necessary evidence; restrict or remove content and accounts proportionately.
5. Notify affected users of the decision and reason where lawful and safe.
6. Log any report to police, NCA, Ofcom, ICO or another authority.
7. Appeals should be reviewed by someone not responsible for the first decision where practicable.
8. Target acknowledgement: priority within 24 hours; standard within seven days. Target final response: 28 days.
9. Retain closed support/safety records normally for two years, longer for live disputes, safeguarding or legal duties.
10. Review aggregate patterns quarterly during public testing and update risk assessments after significant incidents.
