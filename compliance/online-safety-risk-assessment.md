# The Girls Trip Guide — UK Online Safety Act risk assessment

Assessment date: 4 September 2026  
Provider: Storystone Ltd (company 16922351)  
Service type: UK-linked user-to-user file-sharing and information-sharing service  
Products assessed: Free Trip and Full Trip  
Review: at least annually and before a significant service change

## Service evidence

- Private, invitation-only trip groups; no public feed, discovery, follower system or anonymous public posting.
- Users can enter trip details, costs, notes and messages and can upload documents, images and videos.
- Full Trip includes a Hidden Gallery and up to 20 GB of media for 12 months.
- Organisers control invitations and member removal.
- Storystone Ltd can inspect reported content and restrict accounts using authorised owner controls.
- Public reporting and appeal route: /safety and /contact.
- Service is marketed for adults and the Terms require users to be 18 or over.

## Required measures and implementation record

- Terms state prohibited illegal content and available enforcement action.
- /safety explains reporting, triage, removal, complaints and appeals.
- /contact contains dedicated safety, complaint/appeal and refund categories.
- Reports must be logged with received time, content reference, risk decision, action, reviewer and appeal outcome.
- Urgent reports involving immediate danger, CSEA, credible threats or non-consensual intimate imagery receive priority.
- Access may be restricted while evidence is preserved and a report is assessed.
- Account/content action must be proportionate and reasons given where lawful.
- Material product changes require a fresh assessment before release.
- Safety records must not contain copied CSAM.

## Remaining corporate dependencies

1. Storystone Ltd must designate a named UK safety lead and deputy.
2. Register for the National Crime Agency CSEA Industry Reporting Portal and keep the reporting route operational.
3. Confirm that hello@thegirlstripguide.com and the contact-form destination are monitored every working day.
4. Keep a moderation/report log and conduct quarterly sampling during public testing.
5. Reassess intimate-image abuse and cyberflashing after the first 90 days of real usage.

## Decision

The service can enter controlled public testing only after the corporate dependencies above are completed. Intimate-image abuse and cyberflashing remain the principal product risks.
