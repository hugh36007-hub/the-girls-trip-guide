# The Girls Trip Guide — children’s access and risk assessment

Assessment date: 4 September 2026  
Provider: Storystone Ltd  
Review: annually, after material change, or if evidence of child use appears

## Access conclusion

The service is intended for adults and the Terms require all users to be 18 or over. However, invitations can technically be forwarded and there is no highly effective age-assurance system. On the cautious UK approach, Storystone Ltd should treat the service as **likely to be accessed by children** until evidence supports a different conclusion.

## Child risk profile

| Risk | Level | Current mitigation |
|---|---:|---|
| Sexual or intimate imagery, including cyberflashing | Medium | Private groups, adult-only rules, accessible reporting, removal and account restriction. |
| Bullying, threats or coercion within a known group | Medium | Organiser removal, operator complaints route and appeal process. |
| Content encouraging self-harm | Low | No public feed, search or recommendation system; report and removal route. |
| Hate or abusive content | Low | No amplification; prohibited by Terms and removable on report. |
| Grooming or contact by unknown adults | Low | Invitation-only access and no stranger discovery; forwarded invitations remain a risk. |
| Dangerous drug or alcohol content | Low | Adult positioning, no public discovery or recommendation system. |
| Financial pressure or fraud | Low | Cost records only; no member-to-member money handling. |
| Privacy harm from photos, documents or trip location | Medium | Private access, signed media links, organiser controls and deletion/report routes. |

## Controls

- Adult-only statement must appear in Terms and checkout.
- Do not market the service to children.
- Treat any report involving a person under 18 as urgent.
- Remove or restrict content/accounts where required to protect a child.
- Do not request passport scans, full payment-card data or unnecessary sensitive data.
- Keep invitation and media access private by default.
- Record child-safety decisions and review patterns during testing.

## Escalation

Immediate danger: 999. Non-emergency police: 101. Suspected CSEA must follow the NCA reporting process without copying or forwarding illegal material. Reports: hello@thegirlstripguide.com or /contact.

## Decision

Child-protection duties are treated as applicable on a precautionary basis. Reassessment requires evidence about actual users, forwarded invitations, safety reports and the effectiveness of the adult-only controls.
